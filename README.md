# 📊 Retail Business Intelligence Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Retail Business Intelligence Platform** is a data analytics project developed to analyze retail sales data and generate meaningful business insights. The project combines **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to clean, analyze, and visualize sales data through an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze retail sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Compare sales across different regions.
- Understand customer segment performance.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- SQL
- Power BI
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📂 Project Files

- `Retail_Analysis.ipynb` – Data cleaning, EDA and analysis
- `Retail_Analysis.html` – Exported notebook
- `Retail_Sales_Dashboard.pbix` – Power BI dashboard
- `train.csv` – Dataset used for analysis

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- 📌 Total Sales KPI
- 📌 Sales by Region
- 📌 Sales by Category
- 📌 Sales by Segment
- 📌 Top Selling Products
- 📌 Interactive Filters

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Technology category generated the highest sales.
- Consumer segment contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Sales performance varied across regions, highlighting opportunities for business growth.
- Interactive dashboards enable quick business decision-making.
